About us

The Blagodarya.BG Foundation ("Thank You BG") was established in 2008 by Tatiana and Dobrin Dobrevi as a charitable non-profit organisation.

We believe kindness is the shortest path to the heart — and that a community is strong when it keeps its spiritual roots and cares for its most vulnerable children.

Our goals

Preservation and dissemination of spiritual and cultural values, and support for children in need.

Completed initiatives

Children's home — Popovo

Support for a home for disadvantaged children.

Kovachevsko Kale

Funding of archaeological excavations of the fortress.

Nativity of the Theotokos chapel

Construction works on the chapel.

The foundation is funded entirely by donations. All funds go directly to the causes — no administrative salaries, no intermediaries.
